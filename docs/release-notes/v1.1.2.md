Fixes, mostly for Windows.

## Windows

- The level meters in Settings → Audio work. They showed "level element missing".
- Rendering an edit with sound works again.
- Rename, star and label clips in the viewer. These were greyed out for every clip.
- Choosing a hotkey that Windows won't allow (another app owns it) no longer takes away the one you had.
- "Saved" shows the clip's name rather than its whole path.

## Everyone

- Starring, renaming or labelling a clip while trimming keeps your trim.
- New edits and long clips get their thumbnail and length straight away, not after a restart.
- When capture fails mid-game, the header says it's retrying instead of still showing that it's recording.
- The storage limit keeps starred clips. Turn this off in Settings → Storage → Protect starred clips.
- The storage limit carries on when it can't remove a clip, instead of stopping.
- Long edits, such as a 10-minute replay or slow motion, no longer fail after 10 minutes of rendering.
- A settings file that is partly unreadable no longer resets all your settings.
- Opening Settings → Audio no longer stalls the window for a moment.

## Linux

- Picking an audio device no longer risks a crash on older PipeWire versions.

Bugs: [open an issue](https://github.com/lpears0n/reruns/issues) with your log (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\gg.reruns.app\logs` on Windows).
