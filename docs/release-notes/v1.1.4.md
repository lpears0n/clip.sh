NVIDIA recording that keeps working, clips that save even when Windows blocks your Videos folder, and Reruns on your Discord profile.

## Windows

- The NVIDIA encoder keeps working after the first game. Opening Reruns' window broke it until you restarted Reruns, so every later game recorded on your other graphics chip or on Windows' own encoder.
- When Windows' ransomware protection (Controlled folder access) won't let Reruns save in Videos, clips now go to a Reruns folder in your user folder instead of failing, and a notification says so. The same happens for any clips folder Reruns can't write to, such as a drive that's unplugged. Pick your folder again in Settings → Storage once it works.

## Everyone

- **Discord status:** while Reruns records, your Discord profile shows "<game> with Reruns", "Clipping <game>" and how many clips you've saved this session, with the game's icon. For the icons, Reruns downloads Discord's list of games about once a week while this is on. Turn it off with Show on Discord in Settings → General.
- Reruns looks for updates as soon as it starts, instead of 20 seconds later.
- Open your log folder from Settings → General → Troubleshooting, or from the tray menu.

Bugs: [open an issue](https://github.com/lpears0n/reruns/issues) with your log (Settings → General → Troubleshooting → Logs).
