Steadier game audio, a bigger in-game popup, and one Reruns at a time.

## Windows

- Game audio no longer drops out for a moment when the game keeps your PC busy, such as while a match loads.
- The in-game popup is bigger and scales with your screen, so it reads the same at 1080p, 1440p ultrawide or 4K. It has a new look too: its own icon for clips, screenshots and markers, and the hotkey shown as a key.
- When the NVIDIA encoder won't start, Reruns tries it again at the next game, instead of recording on your other graphics chip until you restart Reruns. While another encoder records, the top bar says which one (for example "on AMD AMF"). Hover it to see why.

## Everyone

- Opening Reruns twice in quick succession no longer starts two copies that both record. The second launch brings up the first.

Bugs: [open an issue](https://github.com/lpears0n/reruns/issues) with your log (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\gg.reruns.app\logs` on Windows).
