If you have 1.0.0, Reruns updates itself: Settings → General → **Check now**, then **Update and restart**.

## Share clips

- **Copy a clip** and paste it into Discord: the Copy button on a clip, or Ctrl+C in the viewer.
- **Drag a clip** out of the window into Discord, a chat or a folder.

## Edit

- **Vertical 9:16** for TikTok, Shorts and Reels. Trim → Format → Vertical, then drag the frame to choose what's kept.
- **Slow motion** at ½× or ¼×. Trim → Slow-mo, then set the striped range on the timeline. That range is muted.

## Audio

- **Live levels** in Settings → Audio show what each device picks up, or why it can't be opened.
- The log now says why a track is missing from a clip.

## Look

- A real icon.
- On Windows, the header is the title bar.
- The sidebar folds away in Settings, the viewer and narrow windows.
- A smooth playback bar.
- Shorter text throughout.
- Friendlier dates: "Today, 10:20 PM".

## Recording

- A failing capture shows "Can't record <game> · retrying" instead of "Waiting for a game".
- Windows: if the graphics encoder refuses to start, Reruns switches to the next one that works.

Bugs: [open an issue](https://github.com/lpears0n/reruns/issues) with your log (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\gg.reruns.app\logs` on Windows, `~/.local/share/reruns/logs` on Linux).
